Upgrade

Shield contributes half its 1H upgrade cost to the transfer budget. The resulting 2H upgrade level is the highest achievable with that combined budget.

Refine

For Adv Transfer from Shield to 2H, ensure refine is only on the shield and quality (White/Blue/Gold) must match for all eq.

Enchant

Adv Transfer defaults to the lowest city if 1H and Shield cities differ.

Example:
Shield: Geffen Lv.14 | 1H: Empty → Geffen 2H Lv.10
Shield: Geffen Lv.14 | 1H: Izlude Lv.1 → Izlude 2H Lv.15
