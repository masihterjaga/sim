RöXtimizer

NOTE!

Make sure the Divinity, Card & Companion you're using have the same offensive stats (and available here) as in-game. Don't swap 'em out once you've already input your stats. Unlike in-game, this tool won't reflect stat changes in real-time.

How to Use
1.Replicates the in-game divinity screen with some adjustments. Tap any slot to open its panel.
2.3 dots above the title to set your div quality! You can have multiple sepir & riper flash here!
3.Top right arrows to switch slots. Bottom left arrows to switch between divinities in the same slot.
4.You can add up to 8 divinities per slot with + Add Div. Hit Use to mark which one is currently equipped.
5.All divs added here will be used by the optimizer to find the most effective slot combination.
6.Lock a div to protect it from being deleted or replaced by the optimizer.
7.No worries, nothing resets on refresh or tab close. All your divs & stats are saved locally in your browser until you clear site data.
8.This tool only supports main-offensive stats. Stat/%, HP%, ATK%, Haste not included! So yeah, it might not be acurate for ED ED ED / SB SB SB enjoyer!
